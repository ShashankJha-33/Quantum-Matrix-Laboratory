Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you book.

Do you offer home sample collection?

Yes, home collection can be requested for eligible tests and locations. Availability and charges, if any, are confirmed during booking.

Do I need to fast before a test?

It depends on the test. Some profiles may require fasting. We will confirm preparation requirements when your booking is reviewed.

How will I receive my report?

Reports can be shared digitally through the agreed channel. Report timing varies by test and processing laboratory.

Can I book cancer-related tests?

Yes, specialised and oncology-related laboratory tests can be requested. These should be selected based on clinical advice; a laboratory test alone does not establish a cancer diagnosis.

Is Quantum Matrix a physical laboratory?

This website is designed for a diagnostic booking and sample-collection service. The actual processing laboratory should be disclosed to the customer where applicable.

Can I ask for a custom test list?

Yes. Send the prescription or test list on WhatsApp and the team can confirm availability, price and preparation requirements.
